# Digital-Book-Assets-Manager

### By: Sasa Protic

#### Date: 10/29/2021
***

### ***Description***
####  This is a MEN (MongoDB, Express.js, Node.js) workflow management application designed
####  for editors overseeing digital educational textbooks. The system allows editors to track, coordinate, 
####  and assign requests for the preparation and processing of multimedia assets—such as images, videos, and audio 
####  files—destined for digital publications.
***

### ***Getting Started***

#### 1. Landing Page & Sign-In
* When you first open the application, you are greeted with a clean and modern landing page.
* Click Sign In in the top navigation bar to log into your account using your assigned username and password.
* Smart Alert Banner: Upon logging in, if there are any pending tasks or requests requiring your attention 
  (statuses such as Pending or Needs Clarification), a prominent blue alert banner with a pulsing counter will 
  appear right at the top. It displays the exact number of active tasks waiting for you. Clicking the "Review requests" 
  button will take you straight to your task list.

 #### 2. Request Management (Requests)
* Navigating to Requests opens a list of active tasks tailored to your role.
* Editors / Admins: Can create new requests (New Request), select a publication, attach resource 
  links, and assign the task to a specific processor. They also have permission to manage or delete requests.
* Processors: Can view all tasks specifically assigned to them. By clicking on an individual request, they can 
  view details, open supporting files, update the task status (e.g., In Progress, Needs Clarification, or Completed),
  and leave notes or feedback in the comment section.

#### 3. Publications Overview (Publications)
* The Publications section provides a complete catalog of individual releases, textbooks, and supporting resources.
* Each card displays key metadata, including grade level, subject, title, and assigned owner/editor.
* Authorized editors and admins can also register new publications or remove outdated ones here.

#### 4. Signing Out
* When you finish your session, simply click Sign Out in the top-right corner to securely close your session.
